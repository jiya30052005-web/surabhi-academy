Our Story

Teaching beyond examinations.

We believe coaching shouldn't be about cramming — it should build understanding that lasts well past the exam hall. Every batch at Surabhi Academy is kept small so that no student is just a number in a crowded classroom.

From Class 8th foundation batches to focused IIT-JEE, NEET and MHT-CET target batches, our approach stays the same: clear concepts, honest feedback and a revision routine students can actually stick to.
